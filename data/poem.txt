The Lantern in the Rain

When evening laid its silver veil
Across the quiet lane,
A little lantern kept its glow
And smiled into the rain.

The rooftops hummed a gentle tune,
The old trees bowed their heads,
While rivers of reflected gold
Ran softly past the sheds.

A traveler paused beside the gate
And watched the storm clouds part;
The lantern did not stop the rain,
But warmed the traveler's heart.

So when the road grows dark and long
And hope feels faint again,
Remember: even a modest light
Can shine through heavy rain.
